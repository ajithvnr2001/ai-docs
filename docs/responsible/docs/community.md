# Join the Google Developer Community

> Source: https://ai.google.dev/responsible/docs/community/

Are you interested in chatting with Googlers and other developers about responsible AI Practices?

Join the [Google Developer Community](https://discord.gg/google-dev-community) Discord server! This is a great place to interact with developers, and to learn, share, and support each other. You can find us in the `#gemma`, `#research`, and `#ai-general` channels. See you there!

For more local connections, you can join a [Google Developer Group](https://developers.google.com/community/gdg) (GDG) in your area. There are over 1000 GDGs across 140 countries that offer the chance to connect with others near you on a range of topics, including GenAI, responsibility, and more general developer topics.
