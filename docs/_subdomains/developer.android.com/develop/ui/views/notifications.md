Notifications allow your app to provide users with timely information and reminders even when the app isn't explicitly in use. In View-based apps, you use the Android system APIs and `NotificationCompat` to create and post notifications.

For a comprehensive overview of the Android notification system, including where notifications appear, their anatomy, and system-wide behaviors, see the [About notifications](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/notifications) guide.
