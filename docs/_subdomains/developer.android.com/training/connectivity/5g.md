###  Transforming your app with 5G 

Indoor > Outdoor

###  [ Turn indoor use cases into outdoor use cases ](https://developer.android.com/training/connectivity/5g/enhance-with-5g#indoor-outdoor)

Video chat on the go. Download what you need when you need it. Play multi-player games wherever you happen to be. 

Photo AR

###  [ Turn photo-centric UX into video-centric or AR-centric UX ](https://developer.android.com/training/connectivity/5g/enhance-with-5g#photo-ar)

Upgrade your users' experience by replacing photos with videos and by incorporating augmented reality into your UX. 

Prefetch

###  [ Prefetch helpfully: delight rather than buffer ](https://developer.android.com/training/connectivity/5g/enhance-with-5g#prefetch)

Use the increased bandwidth to prefetch content, further enhancing the responsiveness of your app. 

Niche

###  [ Turn niche use cases into mainstream use cases ](https://developer.android.com/training/connectivity/5g/enhance-with-5g#niche)

Streaming content on older networks was the exception. Make streaming content the new mainstream with 5G.
