Hi Everyone,
Starting June 19, 2026, the Gemini API will stop accepting requests from unrestricted API keys to prevent unauthorized usage and billing risks.
If your Google Cloud project has the Gemini API enabled and contains unrestricted keys, please restrict them immediately.
###  [](https://discuss.ai.google.dev/t/subject-feature-suggestion-eco-mode-model-efficiency-transparency-for-gemini/184311#p-311902-what-to-do-1)What to do:
  * Option 1 (Restrict existing keys): Go to [Google Cloud Credentials](https://console.cloud.google.com/apis/credentials), select your key, and under API restrictions, restrict it to Gemini API ([generativelanguage.googleapis.com](http://generativelanguage.googleapis.com)).
  * Option 2 (New keys): Generate new restricted keys in [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey) and update your code.


Note: Keys generated directly in AI Studio are restricted to the Gemini API by default.

Hello Google AI Product Team,
The computational cost and energy efficiency of large-scale AI inference are among the most critical challenges facing our industry. Google Gemini is already leading the way with its highly efficient architectures, such as the Flash and Gemma model families.
However, as daily users, we notice that many routine tasks (such as basic rewrites, simple summaries, or quick translations) do not require maximum computing power. These could be handled by much lighter, faster, and more energy-efficient models without compromising user experience.
We propose integrating a user-facing **“Eco Mode”** combined with a **Compute Effort Transparency Indicator** within the Gemini mobile and web interfaces. This is not about claiming to make AI “100% green,” but about offering a concrete option for digital sobriety and optimizing infrastructure utilization.
###  [](https://discuss.ai.google.dev/t/subject-feature-suggestion-eco-mode-model-efficiency-transparency-for-gemini/184311#p-334783-core-features-1)Core Features:
  1. **Compute Intensity Selector (UX)**  
As shown in the attached mockup, the mode selector could feature:


  * ![:recycling_symbol:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/twitter/recycling_symbol.png?v=15) **Eco Mode** : Forces the use of lightweight models (e.g., Gemma 2B or a Flash-Lite variant) for simple queries, guaranteeing fast and low-compute execution.
  * ![:balance_scale:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/twitter/balance_scale.png?v=15) **Standard (Default)** : Dynamic, balanced routing.
  * ![:brain:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/twitter/brain.png?v=15) **Extended Reasoning** : Dedicated to complex problem-solving and deep analysis.
  * ![:robot:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/twitter/robot.png?v=15) **Automatic** : Smart selection based on query complexity.


  1. **Inference Transparency**  
Display a discrete badge after generating a response (e.g., “![:recycling_symbol:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/twitter/recycling_symbol.png?v=15) Response generated in Eco Mode · Light compute”). An optional “Compute Effort & Impact” detail window could show qualitative indicators (light compute, minimal carbon footprint, optimized cooling) rather than unverified exact numbers.


###  [](https://discuss.ai.google.dev/t/subject-feature-suggestion-eco-mode-model-efficiency-transparency-for-gemini/184311#p-334783-expected-benefits-2)Expected Benefits:
  * **Infrastructure & TPU Optimization (Google)**: Offloads high-density TPU clusters from trivial queries, freeing up valuable capacity for complex extended reasoning and enterprise workloads.
  * **User Control & Sustainability (Users)**: Gives environmentally conscious users and enterprise clients (Scope 3 digital footprint management) a tangible way to adapt their compute consumption.
  * **Showcasing Google’s Efficient Engineering** : Highlights the performance and practical utility of Google’s lightweight models (Gemma/Flash) directly to the end-user.


We have created a complete conceptual mockup on a Google Pixel following Material Design 3 guidelines (see attached image) to show how natively this could integrate into Gemini.
