[Holywater](https://www.holywater.tech/) is an entertainment network that produces high-quality content using its powerful suite of AI tools. The company has specialized apps covering entertainment categories, including e-books, music, and vertical video with [My Drama](https://my-drama.com/). Its audience of over 55 million users has consumed a combined total of more than 100 million hours of content. 

To keep up with its continually growing audience, the Holywater team needed to rapidly scale its content analysis, content generation, and automation workflows. They selected [Veo 3](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/video) for its high-fidelity video generation and Gemini 2.5 Pro for its complex multimodal understanding, eliminating creative bottlenecks and accelerating video releases. 

## Enhanced content analysis and development with Gemini 2.5 Pro

The Holywater team found that Gemini 2.5 Pro was more consistent and accurate at extracting structured insights from video content than other frontier models. So they began integrating the Gemini API and Google AI Studio into their workflows, with cofounder and co-CEO Anatolii Kasianov noting, “The first usable pipelines were running within a few days, and full operational adoption took less than two weeks.” 

The model helped the team accelerate their content analysis, as the model’s 1 million token context window enables processing of subject matter—such as an entire book series—to understand nuanced elements like plot structure, character development, and emotional tone. It uses advanced reasoning to segment videos into scenes and extracts metadata such as emotional tones, visual cues, and overall context to provide structured insights for creative analysis and automation workflows. 

It then generates narrative structures, scripts, and campaigns. Automating this foundational analysis helps to enable the creatives at Holywater to focus on high-level tasks, such as overall quality control, content strategy, and brand alignment. 

> “After adopting Gemini 2.5 Pro , we saw a significantly faster time-to-production and the removal of manual work for simple or repetitive tasks. This allowed us to automate large parts of our workflow and free up human resources for more complex creative and analytical work.” 

— Anatolii Kasianov, Co-CEO and Cofounder of Holywater

## Delivering next-level visuals with Veo 3

Veo 3 is Google's state-of-the-art model for generating high-fidelity video and audio, accessible through the Gemini API. To take its content creation to the next level, Holywater began implementing Veo 3 into its production pipeline, starting with its My Drama app. 

The team used Veo 3 to generate an entire series based on the structured inputs provided by Gemini 2.5 Pro. They saw early success in the true crime genre, where Veo 3 generated high-quality vertical video that achieved engagement metrics similar to filmed original content. 

![Analytics show that Holywater’s AI-generated series receives up to 22% higher completion rate than filmed series](https://www.gstatic.com/aistudio/case_studies/holywater/series-completion-inline-original.png)

## Reimagining the future of AI entertainment

By automating content analysis and generation using Gemini and Veo models, Holywater is able to release 4-6 new entries each month and have the team focus on analytical tasks. The team is testing Gemini 3 Pro’s video processing capabilities to analyze more frames per second and achieve an even deeper understanding of content. 

My Drama was the Webby Awards 2025 People’s Voice Winner for Best Streaming Service and in October 2025, [Fox Entertainment took an equity stake in Holywater](https://www.holywater.tech/blog/fox-entertainment-enters-a-strategic-partnership-with-holywater-to-drive-hollywoods-evolution-2), committing to produce more than 200 titles for My Drama over the next 2 years. With the growing audience for vertical video, Hollywood is taking notice of ways to scale IP led content strategy. “AI is at our core,” said Kasianov. “It’s not experimental; it’s driving real business growth.”
