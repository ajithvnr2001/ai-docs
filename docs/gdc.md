### Vertex AI Platform

Vertex AI is a fully managed and secure platform for generative AI, offering access to a “model garden” of over 130 generative AI models, all of which can be securely tuned and customized for your specific needs, and then deployed to your development team with ease.

  * [ Learn more ](https://cloud.google.com/vertex-ai)

### Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE)

Train and serve AI models alongside game servers with GKE, our production-ready fully-managed Kubernetes platform that is performant, scalable, cost-effective, and secure. Through GKE, you can choose performance-optimized hardware, such as GPUs and TPUs, and a variety of distributed computing frameworks, such as Ray.

  * [ Learn more ](https://cloud.google.com/kubernetes-engine/docs/integrations/ai-infra)
